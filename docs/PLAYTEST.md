# Closed Playtest Guide

Thanks for testing Ricochet Depths. A run takes about six to eight minutes. Please play at least
three runs, and at least one of them with another person.

## What to do

1. **Play solo first.** Start from the lobby, reach the Warden Vault, and see what happens.
2. **Play at least one run in co-op** with 2–4 players.
3. **Play one run on a phone or tablet** if you can.
4. **Deliberately lose one run.** Let the Chasers get you and see how that feels.

Do not read anything before you play. Part of what we are testing is whether the game explains
itself.

## What we most want to know

Answer these after you finish, in your own words. Short answers are fine.

**First minute**
1. Did you work out how to fire without being told? How long did it take?
2. When did you land your first ricochet — a shot that bounced and then hit something? Did it
   feel good, or accidental?
3. Was anything about the controls confusing or fighting you?

**The core loop**
4. Did bank shots feel like the smart play, or did shooting straight work just as well?
5. The Bulwark blocks shots from the front. Did you work that out yourself? Was it satisfying or
   irritating once you did?
6. Was the run the right length? Too long, too short, about right?

**Upgrades**
7. Did the three cards feel like a real decision, or was one obviously best?
8. Was any card's description unclear?
9. Did you notice your upgrades changing how the game played?

**Co-op**
10. Did playing together work, or did you get in each other's way?
11. When one of you went down, was it clear what had happened and what came next?
12. Did anyone have to wait around for someone else?

**Overall**
13. Would you play another run right now? Why or why not?
14. What is the single thing you would change first?
15. What is the single best moment you had?

## Reporting a bug

Open an issue with:

- **What happened** and **what you expected**.
- **Steps to reproduce**, if you can find them.
- **Where**: room number and room name, shown at the top of the screen.
- **Build**: the string at the bottom of the Options panel.
- **Platform**: desktop, phone or tablet, and how many players were in the run.
- A screenshot or clip if you have one.

Please report these immediately, as they are the most damaging:

- The run gets stuck and you cannot continue.
- Your salvage total is wrong after a run, or you were paid twice.
- A projectile leaves the room, or an enemy walks through a wall.
- The game stops responding, or the frame rate collapses.
- Anything that lets you do something the game clearly did not intend.

## Known issues

Please do not report these; they are already on the list.

- There is no audio yet.
- Enemies do not walk around pillars; they can bunch up against cover.
- Visual effects are placeholders.
- Nothing carries over between runs yet except your salvage total.

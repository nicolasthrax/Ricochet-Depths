## Title

Ricochet Depths

## Description

⚡ BANK YOUR SHOTS. CHAIN YOUR KILLS. ESCAPE THE DEPTHS. ⚡

Drag to aim, release to fire. Your shots bounce off every wall, so line up the perfect bank
shot to hit enemies behind cover, break shields from behind, and chain several kills with one
ricochet.

🔫 Fight through the Ruins, the Foundry and the Abyss, then face the Warden Prime.
🃏 Pick one of three upgrade cards after every room to build your run.
👥 Play solo or team up with 2–8 friends at the Gates.
💰 Collect coins from every kill and spend them on permanent upgrades and trails.
⭐ Level up every run and climb the leaderboards.
🎁 Claim daily rewards, finish daily quests and redeem codes for free coins.

Codes: RICOCHET, DEPTHS, LAUNCH

Like ⭐ and favorite ❤️ the game for updates!
